    Frequently asked questions

    Good to know.

    Are services available in all listed cities?

    The cities show focus and possible expansion markets. Current service availability is not confirmed.

    Are the prices final?

    No. Price ranges are illustrative estimates for this website preview. A real professional would confirm the scope and final quote.

    Can I make a real booking or payment here?

    No. This is a front-end demo. Booking requests are not sent or saved, and no payment is processed.

    How are professionals checked?

    The verification card describes the intended approach. Live onboarding and background checks are not active in this demo.

    Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

    This website is a demo and does not send real bookings. A live service should show cancellation and rescheduling terms before confirmation.
